# Privacy Policy for RTSP Camera

**Last updated:** October 2026

## 1. Introduction
RTSP Camera ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile application that turns your device into a local RTSP IP camera. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and your rights. By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

## 2. Data Collection
RTSP Camera is designed to run entirely on your device and your local network. **We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## 3. Camera & Microphone
The app uses your device's camera and microphone solely to capture a live video/audio stream. This stream is processed on your device and sent only to clients *you* connect on your local network (for example, Home Assistant or VLC). The app does not record, upload, or retain any footage.

## 4. Local Network
The app runs a small RTSP server on your device so other devices on the same Wi-Fi network can connect to the stream. It does not communicate with any external servers or the internet.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because the app collects and stores no data, there is nothing to retain or delete.

## 6. Data Sharing & Third Parties
The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services, and does not share your data with anyone.

## 7. Permissions
To function correctly, RTSP Camera requests the following permissions:
- **Android:** `CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO`.
- **iOS:** `NSCameraUsageDescription`, `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`.

## 8. Children's Privacy
RTSP Camera is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 9. Legal Bases (GDPR/CCPA)
Because the app collects no personal data, no legal basis for processing is required. Camera, microphone, and local-network access are granted through system permission prompts and can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy as RTSP Camera evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

## 11. Contact
For any privacy-related questions, please contact: **support@rtspcamera.app**
